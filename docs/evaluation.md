Results generated by `scripts/benchmark_scale.py`. Raw output committed at
`devnotes/evaluation_log.txt`. C selected by 5-fold CV for each dataset.

## Summary

For each dataset, C is selected by 5-fold cross-validation. Both ellipsoidal
(fast) and empirical (Hit-and-Run MCMC) ambiguity are reported.

| Dataset | d | C (CV) | Acc | Ellip. Amb. | Emp. Amb. | Ellip/Emp | Min ESS |
|:--------|--:|-------:|----:|-----------:|----------:|---------:|--------:|
| BC PCA-10 | 10 | 10.0 | 94.4% | **36.0%** | **27.0%** | 1.3x | 203 |
| BC Full | 30 | 0.5 | 95.3% | **18.4%** | **10.0%** | 1.8x | 60 |
| German Credit | 61 | 1.0 | 70.2% | **85.4%** | **11.4%** | 7.5x | 23 |
| Adult Census | 104 | 0.5 | 77.1% | **79.0%** | **9.2%** | 8.6x | 3 |

**How to read this table.** The ellipsoidal ambiguity is a fast estimate computed
from the ellipsoidal approximation. The empirical ambiguity is an MCMC estimate from
Hit-and-Run sampling against the true-set membership oracle. The Ellip/Emp ratio shows how conservative the ellipsoidal approximation is in these runs.

At **d=10**, the ellipsoidal estimate says 36% and HR estimates 27% -- a 1.3x ratio. The two
agree well, and the ellipsoidal estimate is a fast, useful screen.

At **d=104**, the ellipsoidal estimate says 79% but HR finds only 9.2% -- an 8.6x ratio.
The ellipsoidal estimate is conservative. Critically, the ESS of 3 means the HR
chain has not converged at 500 samples in 104 dimensions. Both the ellipsoidal estimate
(overestimate) and the HR result (underestimate due to poor mixing) should be
interpreted with caution at this dimensionality.

**The lead result is Breast Cancer PCA-10**: 94.4% accuracy with a CV-selected C=10.0,
27% of patients have ambiguous diagnoses estimated by 1000 well-mixed HR samples
(ESS=203), and the ellipsoidal estimate overestimates by only 1.3x.

At d=61, the ellipsoidal estimate overestimates ambiguity by 3-20x. The ratio improves at
larger $\epsilon$ because the ellipsoidal approximation is relatively tighter for
larger sets (the cubic error term grows slower than the quadratic).

## Practical Guidance

**d < 20:** Use the ellipsoidal approximation. It tracks the sampled ambiguity within 1.3-1.6x
and compute in milliseconds. HR is useful for confirmation but not required.

**20 < d < 60:** The ellipsoidal approximation can overestimate by 2-8x.
Use it for fast screening, HR for precise estimates. Allocate
1000+ HR samples for adequate ESS.

**d > 60:** The ellipsoidal approximation is highly conservative (8-20x). HR is necessary for
credible MCMC estimates but requires very long chains (ESS < 10 at 500
samples in d=104). Consider using only the ellipsoidal pathway as a conservative
screening tool, or restrict analysis to a lower-dimensional subspace.
